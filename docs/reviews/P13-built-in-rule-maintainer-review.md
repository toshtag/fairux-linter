## Decision Rules

Stable rules require explicit maintainer approval before P13 closeout. Every
stable record must become `maintainer-approved`; otherwise the strict approval
gate must fail.

Experimental rules require maintainer review and disposition, but they remain
`prepared`, `experimental`, and default-off unless the maintainer explicitly
approves a different disposition.

Uncovered scenarios are part of the approval packet. They are known
non-exhaustive coverage boundaries, not review exceptions. If a source, logic,
or maturity issue is found while reviewing an uncovered scenario, approval must
stop until the issue is resolved.

Review exceptions are currently zero. A stable rule with an open review
exception cannot be closed out.

This approval is review-governance evidence only. It does not make legal,
fraud, compliance, or site-safety claims.
